# Title



Evaluating the Long-Term Effects of AI Reliance on Younger Students



# Summary



This proposal explores how early reliance on AI chatbots like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini across academic learning widens the gap between completed schoolwork and the actual student knowledge that was retained, emphasizing the great need for updating the educational framework to help preserve the students critical thinking skills. 



# Proposal Details



I am studying the effects of the earlier reliance on AI tools among younger students of all academic backgrounds. As AI chatbots such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini grow more powerful and accessible, the younger generations of students have increasingly been able to access these assistants to complete their schoolwork. I want to find out how this early reliance on AI affects their long-term abilities to critically think, work independently, and analyze information. As reliance for AI grows, the gap between the students completed schoolwork and their actual knowledge continuously widens. In order to help my reader understand how the future framework of the education must adapt to retain the authentic learning and critical thinking skills for the future generations, I will explore the shift of when AI began to grow and began to affect younger students with their work. This is crucial to recognize before the future students won’t have the capabilities of completing individual work.
