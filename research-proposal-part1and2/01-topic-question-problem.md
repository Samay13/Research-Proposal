# Title



Chicago Bulls Roster Transition Analysis



# Summary



This proposal explores how the Chicago Bulls transitioned from an older veteran team into a team with a younger core with limitless potential.



# Proposal Details



I am studying the strategic roster changes the Chicago Bulls made in recent years. Throughout the 2021-2022 season, high expectations surrounding the team quickly vanished due to injuries, which caused them to drop from the first to the sixth seed. Eventually that era came to an end and a couple of years later, the Bulls needed to begin their rebuild. They shifted away from the old veteran core of Zach LaVine, DeMar DeRozan, Lonzo Ball, and Alex Caruso towards a younger core of Matas Buzelis, Coby White, and Josh Giddey. I want to find out all the specific details in which the Bulls front office moved pieces around to be able to make this shift. In order to help my reader understand how NBA teams can successfully transition from a mid-tier play-in team into a playoff contending team, I will break down the trades and offseason moves into parts to help reveal all the inner workings of each move and the overall impact each one has.
